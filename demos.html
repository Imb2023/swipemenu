WHAT I’D START WITH

Three offers. One clear favorite.

Try one drink, one food special, and one extra or dessert. Three is a practical starting hypothesis for a quick specials viewer—not a scientifically proven sales optimum. One works for a single promotion; five is useful to test whether extra choice helps or slows guests down.

Show the essentials first

Keep the photo, serving description, and price together. Don’t hide the price or require a video to understand the offer.

Keep the images small

Every photo here is a local WebP. List layouts use 240px thumbnails; photo-led layouts use 800px versions. Other designs load their images only when opened.

Confirm it with guests

Compare how quickly guests can name a favorite and find its price. Track actual requests across similar shifts before declaring a winner.

Demo assets and sizes

    New food and lime-drink images are generated illustrations of sample offerings. Check real portions, ingredients, prices, currency, and photo rights before using any demo as a live offer.
